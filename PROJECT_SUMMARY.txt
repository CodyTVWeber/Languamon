🎮 GAME FEATURES

Exploration:
  • Tile-based overworld (15x13 grid)
  • Walk through grass for encounters
  • Multiple terrain types

Battle System:
  • Turn-based Pokemon-style battles
  • Quiz questions to catch words
  • 4 actions: Fight, Catch, Run, Use Word
  • Type effectiveness system

Learning:
  • Bidirectional translation quizzes
  • Progressive difficulty by level
  • Visual association with emojis
  • Spaced repetition through gameplay

Collection:
  • Linguodex tracks all caught words
  • 22 words total to collect
  • Statistics and type information
